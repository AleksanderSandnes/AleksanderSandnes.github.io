HMI

Web · iOS · Android · Live

HMI puts your solar panels and your local weather on one screen. It reads production straight from a Growatt inverter and pairs it with your own weather station, so you can see how the sun turned into kilowatt-hours, from today back through the last five years. It runs in the browser and as a mobile app with push notifications.

Next.js · Expo · Spring Boot · Supabase

The Family App

Android · iOS

Everything a household needs to keep track of, in one app the whole family shares. Shopping lists everyone can tick off, a dinner plan for the week, a shared calendar and everyone's birthdays. Wishlists let you reserve a gift without the owner finding out, and there's family chat and a live map of where everyone is. In English and Norwegian.

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · SwiftUI · Supabase

Quire

Web app · Private

A budget that follows your household's own rules. Pick 50/30/20, envelopes or spending caps, and Quire sorts your transactions and warns you before a month goes over.

It also keeps track of recurring bills, savings goals and debt, forecasts the months ahead, and can be shared with everyone you live with.

Catalyst

Web · iOS · Android · In development

A meeting place for the Norwegian startup scene. Founders can find investors, mentors, funding schemes and hubs in one place, and the people backing startups can find the companies worth backing. It's a full rebuild of Leap Glocal, my bachelor project.

Eye-Share Time Export

Chrome · Firefox

For everyone at Eye-Share who logs time in ServiceNow. The hours you record there are copied to your MyTime time card, on the right project and task.

It also keeps a running count of your week, flextime and overtime.

Eye-Share Ticket Export

Chrome · Command line

Saves a ServiceNow case as one tidy document with every comment, work note and attachment, plus the customer's change documents. Read it offline or hand it straight to an AI assistant.

The command-line version does the same from a ticket number, so scripts and agents can use it too.

Gives GitHub Copilot and the support team the full picture of a ServiceNow ticket, attachments and related change documents included, from one central place.
